# #02-publishing · *Owners: Owen + Adoniah (deep) — three tracks in parallel*

*Purpose: run the literary-agent route, the paid hybrid offer on the table, and a self-publish fallback side by side — then choose with eyes open.*

## 📌 Canvas

> We have three live options. We keep them moving in parallel and never sign anything exclusive
> that closes the others down. **Golden rule: a traditional publisher/agent pays *you*; if *you*
> pay *them*, it's a hybrid/paid deal — fine to consider, but scrutinise it.** We converge on one
> track only when we hit the decision criteria below.

## Track A — Literary agents / traditional (no upfront cost — they pay us)

- [ ] **[Adoniah]** **Submission tracker** — log every agent/publisher submitted to: name, date sent, response, next step.
- [ ] **[Owen]** Assemble the **submission package:**
  - [ ] Query/pitch letter
  - [ ] Synopsis (1 page)
  - [ ] Author bio + photo
  - [ ] Sample chapters (from #01)
  - [ ] **Marketing/platform one-pager** (audience, socials, list size, launch plan — proof we can sell)
- [ ] **[Owen]** Keep submitting to a target list of agents that fit the genre.
- [ ] **[Adoniah]** **Follow-up plan** — cadence for staying on their radar without nagging.

## Track B — Paid hybrid offer (on the table — evaluate, don't rush)

> Offer received: publisher will take the book **but we pay to be published**, **royalties split 50/50**.
> This is a *hybrid/paid* deal, not a traditional one. Do **not** sign while Track A submissions are live,
> and **do not sign anything exclusive** that kills the agent route.

- [ ] **[Owen]/[Adoniah]** Get the **full offer + contract in writing** and log it in the tracker.
- [ ] **[Team]** **Evaluation checklist — answers required before considering it:**
  - [ ] Exactly what does the **fee cover** (editing, cover, ISBN, distribution, marketing — itemised)?
  - [ ] **Who owns the rights and the ISBN** — us or them?
  - [ ] **Exit clause / term length** — can we get rights back, how and when?
  - [ ] Is it **exclusive**? Does signing kill the agent submissions?
  - [ ] What's the **real distribution** — bookshops & libraries, or just an Amazon listing we could get ourselves?
  - [ ] What marketing is **contractually guaranteed** vs vaguely promised?
  - [ ] Can they show **comparable titles + actual sales numbers**?
- [ ] **[Team]** **Independent contract review** before signing — Society of Authors (free for members) and/or ALLi (rates hybrid publishers).
- [ ] **[Team]** Compare the **economics** vs Track A (free, they pay us) and Track C (we keep ~100% minus costs).

## Track C — Self-publish (fallback, kept ready)

- [ ] **[Adoniah]** Sort **ISBN**, **cover design**, **interior formatting/typesetting**, **pricing**, and **distribution** as a ready-to-launch path.
- [ ] **[Bree]** *(coordinates with #05 — self-pub launch is Bree's domain)*

## Decision

- [ ] **[Team]** Agree **decision criteria + a date** to choose A, B, or C (so the parallel effort converges instead of running forever).
