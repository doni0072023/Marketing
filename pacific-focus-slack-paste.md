## CHANNEL:  02-publishing   ·   Owners: Owen + Adoniah
═══════════════════════════════════════════════

**① Topic/description**
```
Three tracks in parallel: literary agents / the paid hybrid offer / self-publish. Never sign exclusive while others are live.
```

**② Canvas**
```
We have three live options and keep them moving in parallel — never signing anything exclusive that closes the others down. GOLDEN RULE: a traditional publisher/agent pays YOU; if YOU pay THEM, it's a hybrid/paid deal — fine to consider, but scrutinise it. We converge on one track only when we hit the decision criteria.
```

**③ Checklist — Track A: Literary agents / traditional (they pay us)**
```
[Adoniah] Submission tracker — every agent/publisher: name, date sent, response, next step
[Owen] Submission package: query/pitch letter · 1-page synopsis · author bio + photo · sample chapters (#01) · marketing/platform one-pager
[Owen] Keep submitting to a target list of agents that fit the genre
[Adoniah] Follow-up plan — cadence to stay on their radar without nagging
```

**③ Checklist — Track B: Paid hybrid offer (evaluate, DON'T rush — do not sign while Track A is live)**
```
Offer on the table: they'll publish but WE PAY, royalties split 50/50. This is hybrid/paid, not traditional.
[Owen/Adoniah] Get the full offer + contract in writing; log it in the tracker
[Team] Evaluation checklist — answers required before considering it:
   - Exactly what does the fee cover (editing, cover, ISBN, distribution, marketing — itemised)?
   - Who owns the rights and the ISBN — us or them?
   - Exit clause / term length — can we get rights back, how and when?
   - Is it exclusive? Does signing kill the agent submissions?
   - Real distribution — bookshops & libraries, or just an Amazon listing we could get ourselves?
   - What marketing is contractually guaranteed vs vaguely promised?
   - Can they show comparable titles + actual sales numbers?
[Team] Independent contract review before signing — Society of Authors (free for members) and/or ALLi
[Team] Compare economics vs Track A (free, they pay us) and Track C (we keep ~100% minus costs)
```

**③ Checklist — Track C: Self-publish (fallback, kept ready)**
```
[Adoniah] Platform decision — Amazon KDP vs IngramSpark (vs both); note pros/cons
[Adoniah] Sort ISBN · cover design · interior formatting/typesetting · pricing · distribution
[Bree] Coordinates with #05 — self-pub launch is Bree's domain
```

**③ Checklist — Decision**
```
[Team] Agree decision criteria + a date to choose A, B, or C
[Team] Guardrail: never sign anything exclusive while other tracks are still live
```
